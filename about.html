ABOUT DANIEL SOLAR ENGINEERING

Built on Experience. Focused on Engineering.

A family-owned company built around one goal — never let engineering and permitting be the reason a ready project stalls.

Daniel Solar Engineering is a family-owned company founded by Daniel's father after spending more than a decade working alongside solar installers, contractors, builders, and homeowners throughout the solar industry. During those years, he witnessed firsthand how often projects were delayed — not because of installation, but because of the engineering and permitting process.

Incomplete plan sets, slow turnaround times, inconsistent communication, and repeated permit corrections became common obstacles that prevented otherwise ready projects from moving forward. While installation crews were prepared to begin work, engineering and permitting often became the biggest bottleneck in the entire process.

Recognizing the need for a more reliable solution, Daniel's father founded Daniel Solar Engineering with a simple vision: create a company dedicated exclusively to delivering high-quality solar engineering and permit-ready design. Rather than dividing attention between sales, installations, and engineering, the company was built around allowing each team to focus on what they do best.

As the company continued to grow, Daniel joined the business, helping expand operations while maintaining the same commitment to quality, communication, and customer service that the company was founded on. Today, Daniel and his father work closely with an experienced team of solar designers, licensed Professional Engineers, and permitting specialists who support residential, commercial, and utility-scale solar projects across the United States.

OUR STORY

Engineering should move projects forward — not hold them back.

After years of working with solar contractors and installers, we noticed the same pattern repeating itself.

Sales teams were closing projects.
Installation crews were ready to begin work.
Customers were excited to move forward.
Yet projects often stalled because engineering and permitting took longer than expected.

Many contractors struggled to find dependable engineering partners who could consistently deliver accurate plan sets, respond quickly to corrections, and communicate throughout the permitting process. These delays impacted project schedules, increased costs, and created unnecessary frustration for everyone involved.

Daniel Solar Engineering was founded to solve that problem.

By focusing exclusively on solar engineering, design, and permitting support, we've developed a streamlined workflow that prioritizes accuracy, efficiency, and responsiveness. Every project is carefully reviewed by experienced designers and licensed engineers to help ensure it meets local building codes, utility requirements, and jurisdiction-specific standards before submission.

Whether we're preparing a residential rooftop plan set, engineering a commercial solar installation, or assisting with utility-scale developments, our goal remains the same: provide dependable engineering that helps projects move through permitting as smoothly and efficiently as possible.

OUR MISSION

Reliable, accurate, permit-ready engineering.

Our mission is to provide reliable, accurate, and permit-ready solar engineering that helps contractors, builders, developers, installers, and property owners complete their projects with confidence.

We believe engineering should simplify the construction process — not slow it down. By combining experienced designers, licensed engineers, and a customer-focused approach, we strive to deliver engineering solutions that reduce delays, minimize permit revisions, and help our clients keep their projects moving forward.

At Daniel Solar Engineering, we're proud to remain a family-owned business built on long-term relationships, technical excellence, and a commitment to providing engineering services our clients can rely on for every project, no matter the size.
