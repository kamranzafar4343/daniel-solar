SANTA ROSA FAQ

Santa Rosa Solar Engineering Questions

How long does solar permit approval take in Santa Rosa?

City of Santa Rosa Planning & Economic Development offers online solar permit submittals. Our 48-hour plan sets are formatted to pass online plan check review on first submission.

What utility serves Santa Rosa for solar interconnection?

Santa Rosa electric accounts are served by PG&E (Pacific Gas & Electric). We design 100% compliant PG&E Rule 21 interconnection schematics for residential, commercial, and winery solar systems.
