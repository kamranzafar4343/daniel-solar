St. Petersburg Solar Engineering Questions

Does Daniel's Solar Engineering provide PE stamps in Florida?

Yes! We provide professional structural and electrical engineering stamps by licensed Professional Engineers (PE) registered in the State of Florida for both residential and commercial projects.

What are the interconnection requirements for Duke Energy Florida?

Duke Energy Florida requires detailed three-line electrical diagrams, inverter specifications, battery backup wiring layouts (if applicable), and UL-certified equipment statements. We prepare the complete engineering package for immediate utility approval.
