LA Permit Package Standards

LADBS Title Page: Code compliance statements (2022 CEC / CBC), solar PV specs, and site layout.
Electrical Three-Line Diagram: Service panel rating, conductor sizing, grounding, and disconnects.
Structural Engineering Stamps: Wind load calculations (110 mph LA zone), rafter span analyses, and attachment points.
LADWP Clearance Placards: Solar meter disconnect clearances and emergency shutdown placarding.
LOS ANGELES FAQ

Los Angeles Solar Engineering Questions

How long does LADBS solar plan check approval take?

LADBS offers over-the-counter solar permitting for standard residential systems. Our 48-hour plan sets are formatted specifically to pass LADBS plan check on first submission without corrections.

Do I need an MPU (Main Panel Upgrade) plan set for LA solar installations?

If your existing electrical panel is rated at 100A or 125A and cannot support the 120% busbar rule for solar backfeed, we include full MPU panel upgrade single-line diagrams in your permit package.
