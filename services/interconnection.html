OUR INTERCONNECTION PROCESS

From application to PTO

01

Identify Utility Frameworks

We audit the target utility's specific distributed energy resource (DER) rules, localized fee structures, and current net-metering policies.

02

Assemble Compliance Package

We execute all portal engineering forms, append electrical single-line diagrams (SLDs), attach localized three-line schematics, and compile certification data sheets for submittal.

03

Navigate Technical Review

We directly manage the utility’s technical evaluation phase, resolving distribution safety metrics, equipment capacity questions, and any unexpected engineering redlines.

04

Secure Permission to Operate

Upon successful utility verification, we secure your official, formal PTO documentation, clearing the system asset for immediate grid interconnection and deployment.

FAQs

Common questions about utility interconnection

What is the typical timeline to clear a utility interconnection queue?

Project cycle windows depend entirely on individual utility scale and your specific engineering tier. Standard simplified residential systems typically clear within 2 to 4 weeks, while complex Fast-Track commercial or multi-megawatt distribution systems requiring grid-impact studies can take several months. We supply localized timeline projections during project intake.

Do you manage parallel Net Energy Metering (NEM) enrollment?

Yes. We handle parallel tracking and submittal for all applicable localized net energy metering (NEM) tariff structures, virtual net metering setups, and commercial power purchase agreement (PPA) utility data packets natively within your application package.

How are utility engineering corrections or design rejections handled?

If a distribution engineer issues a technical correction notice or deficiency redline, our layout and coordination desk evaluates the feedback immediately. We realign the electrical drawings or digital documentation to match the requested utility safety parameters and process the resubmittal package swiftly.

What system sizes and capacity classes can your firm process?

Our processing desk manages utility submittals across the entire project spectrum. Our portfolio ranges from localized residential net-metered arrays up to complex Commercial & Industrial (C&I) solar deployments, multi-megawatt configurations, and virtual net-metering clusters paired with high-capacity energy storage profiles.
