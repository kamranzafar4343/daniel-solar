OUR COMMERCIAL DESIGN PROCESS

From concept to a stamped, buildable set

01

Scope & Site Review

We look over your baseline utility goals, map your facility's physical space constraints, and audit your main switchgear or panelboard nameplate ratings to ensure your C&I layout fits your targets perfectly without overloading your service infrastructure.

02

Engineering & drafting

We build out your core electrical single-line diagrams (SLDs), configure optimal string maps, calculate exact conduit fills, and run high-fidelity 3D solar shade optimization models.

03

Stamped, permit-ready set

We compile your comprehensive construction drawing sets complete with itemized equipment manufacturer data sheets. If your project parameters or local jurisdiction require a formal engineering validation, we can bundle active state-specific P.E. stamps right into your package. Most projects are completed in a reliable 2-to-3-week milestone window.

04

Review support & revisions

We track your project all the way through the city plan-check queue and the utility interconnection portal. If an inspector or distribution engineer comes back with questions or custom code redlines, our team jumps on it to update your drawings quickly.

FAQs

Common questions about commercial solar

Optimal Asset Capacity & Production Modeling

System sizing is mathematically balanced against your facility's total physical space constraints and local utility rate structures. We match your building targets against local roof or terrain boundaries to identify the optimal peak capacity threshold that maximizes your internal rate of return (IRR).

Structural Mounting Configurations

Yes. Our design desk handles specialized solar carports, fixed or tracked ground-mount arrays, and flat or pitched commercial roofing structural layouts. Every plan set integrates ancillary sub-system architecture, pairing structural foundations smoothly with centralized electrical inverters, BESS layouts, and localized sub-panel configurations.

Project Timelines & Milestones

Standard Commercial & Industrial design development cycles vary based on total megawatt scale and environmental criteria. Complete, construction-ready drawing packages are typically finalized within a reliable 2-to-3-week milestone window following initial site intake and structural parameter verification.

Engineering Sub-Studies & Add-Ons

Every baseline package delivers complete electrical single-line diagrams, physical layout coordinates, and portal-ready utility documentation. Specialized auxiliary sub-studies—including short-circuit calculation sheets, electrical coordination evaluations, arc flash safety risk assessments, and targeted professional engineering stamps—can be bundled directly into your active project parameters as required by your utility or local building department.
