QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions About Residential Electrical Design

Answers to what homeowners, builders, and contractors most often ask about our residential electrical design service. Don't see what you need? Reach out directly.

01 Do I need electrical plans for an electrical panel upgrade?

Many jurisdictions require electrical plans or detailed permit documentation when upgrading an electrical service, increasing panel capacity, or modifying existing service equipment. Requirements vary depending on your local building department and utility provider.

02 Can you design electrical plans for custom homes, additions, and ADUs?

Yes. We prepare complete residential electrical designs and permit-ready electrical plans for custom homes, home additions, accessory dwelling units (ADUs), remodels, garage conversions, and new residential construction.

03 Do you provide electrical load calculations?

Yes. When required for your project, our team performs National Electrical Code (NEC) electrical load calculations to properly size electrical services, panels, feeders, and other electrical equipment.

04 Do your electrical plans include a Professional Engineer (PE) stamp?

When required by your local jurisdiction or project scope, our licensed Professional Engineers review and provide PE-stamped electrical drawings to satisfy permitting requirements.

05 How long does residential electrical design take?

Project timelines vary depending on the size and complexity of your project. Many residential electrical design projects can be completed within just a few business days after receiving the required project information.

06 What is included in a residential electrical design package?

Depending on your project, your electrical design package may include electrical floor plans, electrical site plans, panel schedules, circuit layouts, single-line diagrams, service calculations, electrical load calculations (when required), grounding details, permit notes, and other documentation required for permit submission.

07 Can you prepare electrical single-line diagrams?

Yes. We prepare clear, code-compliant single-line diagrams that illustrate the electrical distribution system and are commonly required as part of residential permit-ready electrical plans.

08 Can you revise electrical plans if the building department requests corrections?

Yes. If your local building department requests revisions during plan review, our team will work with you to address comments and prepare updated documentation to help move your project toward approval.
