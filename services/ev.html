WHY EV CHARGING ENGINEERING MATTERS

Avoid tripping breakers and failed inspections

Deploying scalable Electric Vehicle Supply Equipment (EVSE) requires strict alignment with NEC Article 625 standards and California Electrical Code (CEC) infrastructure mandates. Our engineering team executes precise main service panelboard calculations and dynamic demand-response load management designs to prevent system distribution failures—eliminating complex local building department plan-check redlines and field installation delays across all jurisdictions.

  • ✓ NEC & CEC Load Analysis – Comprehensive 12-month billing data reviews and continuous load calculation sheets to evaluate upstream transformer and switchgear capacities safely.
  • ✓ Complete Plan Sets – Scalable site plans, detailed electrical single-line diagrams (SLDs), underground conduit/trenching pathways, and comprehensive equipment scheduling.
  • ✓ Fleet & Public EVSE – Specialized electrical schematics tailored for commercial Level 2 clusters, workplace charging stations, industrial fleet vehicle depots, and high-kilowatt DCFC hubs nationwide.
  • ✓ DER Sync & Microgrids – Intelligent engineering strategies coordinating heavy EV charging loads alongside active solar PV networks, automated energy management systems (EMS), and battery storage.

FAQs

Common questions about EV charging design

Do I need a panel upgrade for a Level 2 charger?

Often yes, but not always. We'll determine this based on your existing load and the charger's power requirements.

Can you design for commercial fleet charging?

Absolutely. We handle multi-port Level 2 and DC fast-charging sites, including load management and demand-response capabilities.

How long does the design take?

Typically 48–72 hours for a residential system; commercial projects vary by complexity.

Do you include utility coordination?

Yes – we can prepare the utility interconnection application for your EV charger if needed.

Nationwide EVSE Code Compliance & Grid Integration

Our electrical infrastructure plan sets are engineered to cross-examine hardware configurations against evolving regional power metrics, strict public utility constraints, and localized green building updates across the country.

California · CEC & CALGreen

Title 24 & ALMS Compliance

Engineered to execute complete compliance documentation for the rigorous 2026 CALGreen Title 24 mandates, optimizing Automated Load Management Systems (ALMS) to satisfy the 100% EV-ready/receptacle frameworks for multi-family, non-residential, and commercial parking expansions.

Nationwide · NEC Article 625

National Code Alignment

Precise calculation of continuous electrical loads, overcurrent protection device (OCPD) thresholds, and specialized feeder/branch circuit ampacities to support massive multi-port configurations.

Connector Standards

Hardware Interface Architecture
