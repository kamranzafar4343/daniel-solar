Houston Solar Permitting & AHJ Plan Check Expertise

Navigating the City of Houston Permitting Center (Houston Public Works) and Harris County building codes requires exact compliance with the 2023 National Electrical Code (NEC), 2021 International Residential Code (IRC), and City of Houston Amendments.

At Daniel's Solar Engineering, our licensed Texas Professional Engineers deliver jurisdiction-ready solar and energy storage plan sets tailored specifically to the requirements of Greater Houston AHJs and CenterPoint Energy.

1. High-Wind Structural Engineering (150 MPH Coastal Standards)

Located in the Gulf Coast hurricane zone, Houston solar installations must meet strict ASCE 7-16 wind load standards for basic wind speeds ranging from 130 mph to 150 mph (Exposure B and C):

  • Rafter & Truss Uplift Calculations: Verification of existing roof framing capacity with P.E. wet or digital seals.
  • Fastener Pullout Schedules: Sizing 5/16" lag screws and structural screw attachments into Southern Yellow Pine (SYP) wood trusses.
  • Roof Setbacks & Fire Pathways: Alignment with Houston Fire Department 36-inch ridge and valley access pathways.

2. CenterPoint Energy Interconnection & Net Metering

We prepare complete utility interconnection documentation for CenterPoint Energy and retail electric providers (REPs) operating in ERCOT deregulated territory:

  • Single Line Diagrams (SLD): Clear depiction of CenterPoint bi-directional smart meters, visible-lockable AC disconnect switches, and Point of Interconnection (POI).
  • NEC 705.12 120% Busbar Rules: Engineering supply-side taps or main panel upgrades (MPU) for standard 200A / 400A service entrances.
  • Rapid Shutdown & UL 1741 SB Inverter Compliance: Inverter grid support configurations for Texas ERCOT standards.

3. What is Included in Our Houston Solar Permit Packages?

  • Sheet PV1: Title page, site plan, property boundaries, aerial layout, and AHJ governing codes.
  • Sheet PV2: Roof plan, stringing diagram, module azimuth/tilt, and attachment spacing schedules.
  • Sheet PV3: Electrical 3-Line / Single Line Diagram, wire and conduit deratings, OCPD breaker sizing.
  • Sheet PV4: Safety placard schedule, ANSI warning labels, and rapid shutdown marking details.
  • Texas P.E. Stamps: Electrical and Structural P.E. digital signature & seal for 100% first-pass plan check approval.
