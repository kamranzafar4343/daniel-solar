City of San Diego DSD & County Building Department Compliance

Permitting solar PV systems in San Diego requires strict adherence to local building codes, fire setback requirements, and SDG&E interconnection rules. We prepare 100% compliant CAD plan sets tailored specifically to the City of San Diego Development Services Department (DSD), Chula Vista, Oceanside, Escondido, El Cajon, Carlsbad, and Unincorporated San Diego County.

  • ✅ 48–72 Hour Turnarounds: Full CAD single-line diagrams (SLD), site plans, and attachment details.

  • ✅ SDG&E Interconnection: Seamless utility coordination for fast permission to operate (PTO).

SAN DIEGO FAQ

San Diego Solar Engineering Questions

How long does it take to get a solar permit plan set in San Diego County?

Our engineering team delivers completed, AHJ-ready solar permit plan sets within 48 to 72 hours for all San Diego County jurisdictions, including City of San Diego DSD, Oceanside, Chula Vista, and Carlsbad.

Does SDG&E require special battery storage interconnection approval?

Yes, under California NEM 3.0 rules, SDG&E requires specific battery storage export controls and fast control settings. We engineer full NEM 3.0 storage packages that satisfy SDG&E interconnection guidelines.
