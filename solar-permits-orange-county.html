ORANGE COUNTY FAQ

Orange County Solar Engineering Questions

Where is Daniel's Solar Engineering located in Orange County?

We are headquartered in Irvine, CA at 8583 Irvine Center Dr #326, Irvine, CA 92618. We provide engineering and PE stamped plan sets for all 34 cities in Orange County.

Does City of Irvine require PE stamps for residential solar permits?

The City of Irvine Community Development Department requires structural PE calculations for tile roofs or non-standard rafter spacing, as well as electrical engineering stamps for panel upgrades over 200A.
