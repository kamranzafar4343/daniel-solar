Fontana Solar Engineering Questions

How long does solar permit approval take in Fontana?

City of Fontana Community Development Department offers online solar permit submittals. Our 48-hour plan sets are formatted to pass online plan check review on first submission.

What utility serves Fontana for solar interconnection?

Fontana electric accounts are served by Southern California Edison (SCE). We design 100% compliant SCE Rule 21 interconnection schematics for residential and commercial solar systems.
