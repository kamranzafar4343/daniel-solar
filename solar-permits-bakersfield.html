BAKERSFIELD FAQ

Bakersfield Solar Engineering Questions

How long does solar permit approval take in Bakersfield?

City of Bakersfield offers online expedited solar permits. Our 48-hour plan sets are formatted to pass online plan check on first submission.

Which utilities serve Bakersfield for solar interconnection?

Bakersfield is served by PG&E (Pacific Gas & Electric) and SCE (Southern California Edison) depending on location. We engineer interconnection packages for both utilities.
