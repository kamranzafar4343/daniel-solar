DFW Solar Permitting & Oncor Interconnection Expertise

Serving the City of Dallas Development Services, City of Fort Worth Planning & Development, Plano, Frisco, Arlington, and Irving building departments, our engineering team produces code-compliant permit sets adhering to the 2023 National Electrical Code (NEC), 2021 International Residential Code (IRC), and local North Central Texas Council of Governments (NCTCOG) amendments.

1. Oncor Electric Delivery Interconnection Studies

In the Oncor utility service territory, proper Single Line Diagrams and safety disconnect specifications are required for rapid PTO (Permission to Operate):

  • Smart Meter & POI Specification: Exact electrical layout connecting to Oncor advanced metering infrastructure (AMI).
  • Main Panel Upgrades (MPU) & Supply-Side Taps: Conforming to NEC 705.11 / 705.12 for 100A to 400A split-bus and all-in-one meter panels.
  • Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS): Tesla Powerwall 3, Enphase 5P, and FranklinWH backup panel schematics for Texas grid storm resilience.

2. North Texas Structural & Hail Engineering Standards

North Texas experiences severe convective thunderstorms, straight-line winds, and heavy hail impact. Our structural P.E. engineers verify:

  • 115 – 120 MPH Basic Wind Speeds (ASCE 7-16): Roof rafter moment capacity, point load reactions, and fastener spacing.
  • Tile & Standing Seam Metal Roof Mounts: Certified clamping and flashing details for North Texas luxury residential and commercial buildings.
