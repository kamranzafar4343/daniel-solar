How long does solar permit approval take in Carlsbad?

City of Carlsbad Building Division offers online solar permit submittals. Our 48-hour plan sets are formatted to pass online plan check review on first submission.

What utility serves Carlsbad for solar interconnection?

Carlsbad electric accounts are served by SDG&E (San Diego Gas & Electric). We design 100% compliant SDG&E interconnection schematics for residential and commercial solar systems.
