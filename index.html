QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Quick Answers Before You Start

Need more detail? Contact our team.

01 What is included in a solar permit plan set?

Depending on the project, plans include site and roof layouts, electrical diagrams, equipment details, and required engineering notes or calculations.

02 Can you provide PE stamps?

Yes. Licensed engineers can review and stamp structural or electrical drawings when your jurisdiction or project requires them.

03 How long does a plan set take?

Many residential projects take about one business day; complex projects may take 24?48 hours or longer depending on scope and complete intake information.

04 What do you need to start?

Send the project address, equipment details, available plans, and site or electrical panel photos. We will confirm any missing items.

05 Will you revise plans after AHJ or utility comments?

Yes. We address requested plan-check and utility revisions at no additional cost.

06 How much does solar engineering cost?

Pricing depends on project size, jurisdiction, and stamp requirements. Share your scope for a tailored quote.
