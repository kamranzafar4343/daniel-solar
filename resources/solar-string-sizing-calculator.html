Engineering Formulas Used

V_max = Voc × Modules_per_string × [1 + (Tc × (T_min − 25°C) / 100)]
(NEC 690.7 — Maximum System Voltage)

Where Tc = temperature coefficient of Voc (%/°C), T_min = lowest expected ambient temperature.

I_continuous = Isc × Strings × 1.5625
(NEC 690.8(A) — Continuous Duty Conductor Sizing)

NEC 690.8 requires DC conductors to be sized at 125% of the maximum current, and because this is a continuous load, an additional 125% factor applies: 1.25 × 1.25 = 1.5625.

Max_Solar_Breaker = (Bus_Rating × 1.20) − Main_Breaker
(NEC 705.12(B)(2)(3) — 120% Busbar Rule)

The solar backfeed breaker may not exceed: (Panel bus rating × 120%) minus the main breaker size. If exceeded, a Main Panel Upgrade (MPU) is required.
