What is Included in Every Daniel's Solar Plan Set?

📐 Title & Plot Plan (PV1):

Site layout, aerial setbacks, property boundaries, utility disconnect locations, and Fire Department pathways per IFC 1205.

⚡ Single Line Diagram (SLD):

Full conductor schedules, conduit sizing, OCPD breaker ratings, inverter wiring, and NEC 690/705 120% busbar math.

🏠 Structural Attachment Details:

Rafter/truss span calculations, lag screw pullout resistance, dead/live wind loading per ASCE 7-16, and flashing details.

🏷️ Safety Placards & NEC Labels:

Complete custom label schedule formatted to exact AHJ and utility inspection requirements.
